# 📊 Global Tech Layoffs Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Global Tech Layoffs Analysis** project is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze global workforce reductions across technology companies, industries, countries, and company funding stages.

The project explores historical layoff trends, identifies the most affected companies and industries, and examines geographical distributions to understand global workforce reduction patterns.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to transform raw layoff data into meaningful business insights through visualizations, KPIs, charts, and interactive filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze global technology layoffs.
- Identify yearly layoff trends from 2020 to 2025.
- Identify companies with the highest number of layoffs.
- Analyze layoffs across different industries.
- Compare country-wise layoff distributions.
- Examine layoffs by company funding stage.
- Develop interactive dashboards using Power BI.
- Generate meaningful insights to support business analysis and decision-making.

- Total Employees Laid Off
- Companies Affected

- Countries Affected
- Largest Single Layoff Event

**Visualizations:**

### 2. Industry & Company Analysis

Analyzes the distribution of layoffs across industries and companies.

**Key Insights:**
- Hardware recorded the highest industry layoffs, with 81,428 employees.
- Other and Consumer industries also recorded significant layoffs.
- Intel leads the displayed Top 10 company ranking.
- Microsoft and Amazon follow Intel.

**Visualizations:**
- Industry Share of Total Layoffs
- Top 10 Industries by Layoffs
- Average Layoffs per Event
- Company and Industry Analysis Table

### 3. Geographic & Funding Analysis

Examines layoffs across countries and company funding stages.

**Key Insights:**
- The United States recorded the highest country-level layoffs.
- India ranked second in the displayed country comparison.
- Post-IPO companies accounted for the largest recorded layoffs by company stage.

**Visualizations:**
- Top 10 Countries by Total Layoffs
- Global Layoff Distribution Map
- Total Layoffs by Company Funding Stage
- Country and Funding Stage Filters

## 📈 Key Findings

1. **Year-wise Trends:** 2023 recorded the highest layoffs during the analyzed period, with 264,220 employees affected.

2. **Industry Analysis:** Hardware ranked first among industries, with 81,428 recorded layoffs.

3. **Company Analysis:** Intel recorded the highest layoffs in the displayed Top 10 company ranking, with 38,000 employees.

4. **Geographic Analysis:** The United States accounted for the largest recorded country total, followed by India.

5. **Funding Stage Analysis:** Post-IPO companies recorded the highest absolute layoff total, approximately 439,000 employees.

6. **Global Impact:** The dashboard covers 1,898 companies across 54 countries and 30 industries.

## 🔍 Data Analysis & Methodology

The project follows these major steps:

1. Data Collection – Import the global layoffs dataset.
2. Data Cleaning – Inspect missing values, duplicates, and inconsistent records.
3. Data Transformation – Prepare and organize the dataset using Power Query.
4. Data Modeling – Structure the data for reporting and analysis.
5. DAX Calculations – Create measures for total layoffs, company counts, industry contributions, and other KPIs.
6. Data Visualization – Develop interactive charts, cards, maps, and tables.
7. Dashboard Development – Organize the analysis into three dashboard pages.
8. Business Insights – Interpret the results and identify major layoff patterns.

## ⚠️ Data Limitations

- Funding amounts are missing, so funding-versus-layoff relationships cannot be assessed.
- Company workforce totals are unavailable, so actual workforce-reduction percentages cannot be reliably calculated.
- Country-level values shown in the presentation are approximate dashboard readings.
- The largest single layoff event should be verified using the event-level source records.
- The analysis identifies patterns but does not establish the causes of layoffs.

## 💡 Recommendations

- Monitor workforce reduction trends over time.
- Investigate industries and companies with high recorded layoffs.
- Use country-level trends to identify geographical concentrations.
- Incorporate verified funding information into future analysis.
- Include company workforce totals to calculate actual workforce-reduction percentages.
